
# Mock Interview Reflection

## Practice Details

- **Date of practice:** 9 November 2026
- **Practice format:** Online written mock interview
- **Role practised for:** Junior Data Analyst / Data Analyst

## Interview Questions

- **Questions I answered confidently:**
  1. Tell me about yourself and why you want to become a Data Analyst.
  2. What is data analysis, and why is it important to a business?
  3. Which tools have you used in data analysis?
  4. What is data cleaning, and why is it important?
  5. Explain Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA).
  6. Tell us about a data analytics project you have worked on.
  7. How would you handle missing or duplicate data?
  8. Describe a challenge you have faced and how you handled it.
  9. How would you explain a technical finding to someone without a technical background?
  10. Why should we hire you as a Data Analyst?

- **Questions I found difficult:** No major difficulties were identified during the written practice. Spoken responses have not yet been assessed.

  I reviewed and confirmed answers covering my educational background, IT support experience, data annotation experience, data analytics training and Duka Retail Sales Performance capstone project. I demonstrated an understanding of key data analytics concepts.

  Practise answering the ten questions aloud, using clear explanations and real examples from my projects and work experience.
